# TryHackMe: Junior Security Analyst Intro

**Path:** SOC Level 1
**Platform:** TryHackMe
**Status:** Completed

## Overview
This beginner room walks through a day in the life of a junior security analyst, also called a SOC Level 1 analyst.

## What I did
1. Learned that a junior security analyst works with the SOC (Security Operations Center) team, the defensive Blue Team side of cybersecurity.
2. Reviewed a suspicious IP address and analysed it, which confirmed it was malicious.
3. Escalated the incident to the appropriate team for deeper investigation.
4. Added the malicious IP to the firewall blocklist, with a comment explaining the reason, to stop the threat from spreading.

## What I learned
- A SOC analyst monitors alerts and decides which are real threats.
- Escalating serious incidents to the right team is a key part of the job.
- Clear, factual comments on actions like firewall blocks help the whole team understand what happened and why.

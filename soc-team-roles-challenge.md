# TryHackMe: SOC Role in Blue Team - SOC Team Roles Challenge

**Path:** SOC Level 1
**Platform:** TryHackMe
**Status:** Completed

## Overview
A scenario-based challenge where I matched seven security incidents to the right role in a security team.

## Scenarios and roles
1. SIEM alert about a firewall brute force attempt: **SOC Level 1 analyst**, who triages new alerts and escalates real threats.
2. HR manager launched phishing malware: **SOC Level 2 analyst**, who does deep analysis of confirmed incidents.
3. Ransomware hit the France office: **CERT lead**, who coordinates the response to major incidents.
4. PCI DSS audit for credit card servers: **GRC auditor**, who checks compliance with standards.
5. Checking a new website version for vulnerabilities: **Penetration tester**, an ethical attacker who finds weaknesses first.
6. SIEM unavailable due to storage limit: **SOC engineer**, who maintains the SOC tools and infrastructure.
7. FIN7 threat group targeting the company: **Threat researcher**, who studies attacker tactics and techniques.

## What I learned
- Analysts use the tools, and engineers keep them running.
- Level 1 spots and escalates, while Level 2 investigates in depth.
- A CERT lead steps in for major incidents that need coordination.
- Penetration testers and threat researchers are different roles: one tests systems, the other studies attackers.
